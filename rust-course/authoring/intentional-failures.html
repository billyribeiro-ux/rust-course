Use the investigation loop

State the expected contract, reproduce the observed error, identify the first failing boundary, name two possible causes and choose an experiment that distinguishes them. After fixing your copy, rerun the original case and nearby boundaries. Explain why the fix addresses the cause. Open the playbook and export your notes so another reviewer can challenge the reasoning.
