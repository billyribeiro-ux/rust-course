Bounded LF framing before log grammar

Engineering decisions at this checkpoint

The generic BufRead boundary permits tiny controlled buffers and injected failures. A line keeps at most its configured byte limit; the reader drains excess bytes before returning so the next call begins at a true boundary. A custom byte-at-a-time reader would also express the protocol but introduces more I/O calls unless buffered separately. Tests cover chunk splits, exact bounds, empty lines, final unterminated input, oversized recovery and actual read failure. Grammar validation is deliberately a later responsibility.
